Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2025

Overview

This Chrome extension automates accepting Shopify Admin’s own product category and attribute suggestions by simulating the clicks a merchant would perform in their Shopify Admin. All actions occur locally in your browser. The extension does not transmit data to any external server.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, etc.)
  • No authentication, payment, health, location, or browsing history
  • No keystroke, mouse movement, or page content recording

Permissions & Usage

  • activeTab – runs only when you explicitly enable it on your active Shopify Admin tab.
  • storage – saves local preferences (enabled/disabled, delay settings). Data stays on your device.
  • scripting – injects automation script into Shopify Admin pages to simulate button clicks.
  • Host permissions (https://*.myshopify.com/*) – restricts extension to Shopify Admin only.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Children’s Privacy

This extension is intended for business users and not directed to children under 13.

Security

The extension does not communicate with external servers, minimizing risk of data exposure. We recommend following best practices for browser and Shopify account security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
